## Acceptance Rule

This goal only passes as a full dry-run proof if:

- the original PDF identity is verified;
- a fresh source reconstruction is generated by script;
- the fresh source is equivalent to the accepted v6 source contract or the
  missing deterministic cleanup is implemented and rerun;
- downstream HTML/PDF are generated from the accepted dry-run chain, not copied
  from v6;
- the final report has no blocker findings.

If the fresh source differs from v6, stop and fix `kb_creator`/reused
Translator logic before continuing to publishing.

## Non-goals

- Do not overwrite v6 baseline artifacts.
- Do not claim release approval.
- Do not hide manual/LLM review gaps behind copied accepted outputs.
