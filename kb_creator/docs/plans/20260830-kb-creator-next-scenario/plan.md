### Step 8A contract — Visual asset finalization

This step deliberately occurs after a frozen, QA-complete `MASTER_RU` candidate
and immediately before HTML publication. Step 7 owns EN text fidelity,
hierarchy, semantic art role, logical asset id, owner anchor, source page and
source-region provenance; Step 8 translates and checks the accepted semantic
document without requiring user image choices. Step 8A candidate generation is
also automatic. The user receives the completed RU candidate and `artview.html`
as one bounded checkpoint; HTML and PDF remain fail-closed until both RU and the
Step 8A decision manifest are accepted.

For each reader-art logical id, the candidate builder should produce only the
applicable alternatives:

1. current accepted/reconstruction crop;
2. direct embedded PDF image/XObject associated with the source page/region;
3. deterministic full-page PDF render when the source page is a handout/form or
   when direct extraction cannot preserve a composite page;
4. optional explicitly reviewed derived crop or stitched spread.

Source pages with an accepted non-reader disposition such as
`excluded_advertising` are recorded in the candidate manifest as skipped audit
rows and never become artview choices or publication assets.

The review bundle must be a portable `artview.html` backed by a machine-readable
candidate manifest. Each row shows current and source-derived candidates side by
side, full-resolution links, source-page context and comparable evidence:
dimensions, encoded bytes, colorspace, source PDF/page/xref or bbox, hashes,
upscale/resampling indicators, page coverage and likely OCR-text contamination.
Automated quality scoring may sort or recommend candidates, but cannot make a
semantic publication decision by itself.

Each logical asset receives one explicit radio-style decision, not unrelated
checkboxes: `keep_current`, `replace_with_extracted`, `use_full_page`,
`keep_both`, `suppress`, `needs_manual_work`, or `reopen_masters`. `keep_both`
requires distinct reader roles; otherwise semantic duplicates are forbidden.
`suppress` is valid only for a slot already classified as optional/decorative;
suppressing a required handout/map/form or changing semantic ownership must use
`reopen_masters`, return to Step 7 and regenerate Step 8 rather than silently
diverging from the accepted masters.
Decisions must be exportable/importable as JSON and hash-bound to the source
PDF, MASTER_EN, candidate manifest and candidate files; browser `localStorage`
alone is not durable evidence.

Step 8A completion requires:

- every reader asset resolved and zero `needs_manual_work` or `reopen_masters`
  entries;
- user acceptance of the exported decision manifest;
- materialization of the chosen files without mutating source or accepted
  `MASTER_EN`/`MASTER_RU`;
- visual comparison against source-page renders for direct XObject choices;
- no unreviewed crop/full-page duplicates;
- excluded pages/assets match the exact accepted allowlist and do not appear in
  reader candidates;
- an immutable accepted visual-asset manifest referenced by both master
  packages and consumed by HTML/PDF builders.
