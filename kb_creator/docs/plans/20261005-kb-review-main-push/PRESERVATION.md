DOCX correction and prospective DOCX-only fidelity guard. Three final DOCX
successors remain at their approved versioned working paths; none of the six
human-reviewed originals is overwritten. The reviewed original updates,

code/tests/contracts and compact QA/release receipts are included.

DOCX and recovered raster assets use Git LFS. After clone/pull, run `git lfs pull`;
small LFS pointer files are not the actual documents. Approved source/code/data
bytes use -text attributes so signed SHA-256 records remain stable across hosts.

OCR/master cache, rendered page PNG/contact sheet, pytest root, raw command log,
transcript or synthetic diagnostic package is included. Historical receipt
links to these local audit artifacts do not imply those artifacts are present
in Git. Receipts record completed verification, not a self-contained rerun of
all historical diagnostic images. Current synthetic tests remain in the code.

Legacy absolute paths in immutable evidence identify the reviewed inputs on
the original host. They are not a promise of execution recovery on another
device; relocate inputs explicitly and rebind new operations by exact hashes.
Lost upstream masters remain unavailable. Future human source-page and rendered

local and unstaged. Nine approved retired latest HTML/PDF paths are deletions
in Git; their local files were already sent to Windows Recycle Bin.
