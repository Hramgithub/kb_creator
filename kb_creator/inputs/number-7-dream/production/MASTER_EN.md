## INTRODUCTION

## Is all that we see or seem but a dream within a dream? A Dream Within A Dream by Edgar Allan Poe

The investigators brains have been entombed in migo braincanisters. Mercifully they are unaware of this - at least for the time being - as the migo wish to find out something the investigators know, but can’t remember - the location of a certain Elsie Webb.

The migo have developed ‘memory machines’ that put the canned brains into a dream-state and build a virtual construct that allows the human minds to explore their memories as if they were reliving them. The migo can drop in their own artificial elements to effect and observe the investigator’s actions. It is one of these constructs (Number 7) that the investigators unknowingly find themselves in.

This adventure is designed for 2-5 players. Six pre-generated player-characters are provided at the end of the scenario. All of them have a connection to Elsie and/or her father, Stanwick.

The investigators are already doomed in this adventure, so it does not lend itself to being part of an existing campaign. Cruel Keepers can get the players to create their own investigators should they wish but is advised to generate a connection to Stanwick with them before the game starts.

The adventure is nominally set in the English city of Bristol c. 2020, but the Keeper can change the location and time with little effort.

## BACKGROUND

The migo have harvested human minds for decades and have created a ‘wellness’ organisation as a front for their activities on earth. Elsie – a deeply empathic dreamer, hypnotist, and constantly suffering on behalf of others - joined the wellness organisation, called ‘The Enlightened’, to find some peace of mind. With her abilities she quickly became of interest to the migo who were very keen to experiment on her.

Elsie discovered the true work of ‘The Enlightened’ before they were able to harvest her brain, and she has gone into hiding. She has told no one where she is, not even her own father. She knows the migo can extract knowledge from people’s brains against their will - even knowledge that they don’t even know they have.

The investigators have already lived this adventure, they just can’t remember it. The migo are going to help them with that.

## WHAT HAPPENED TO THE INVESTIGATORS?

Before they were captured by the migo, the investigators were contacted by Elsie’s father, Stanwick, to help him find his daughter. Starting at Stanwick’s bookshop, the investigators then visited Elsie’s flat and after investigating The Enlightened, the PCs discovered Elsie in hiding. Elsie told them the truth about The Enlightened, and that the true centre of operations was a place called ‘The Institute’. Agreeing to be hypnotised by Elsie so they could not reveal her location in the event of capture, the investigators forced their way into The Institute. There they discovered the gruesome truth about the migo mindharvest. Captured by the migo, and knowing too much to simply be let go, the investigators were kept alive, and their brains extracted. They now reside in brain-canisters plugged into Construct 7.

## CONSTRUCT 7

Construct 7 is built from the investigators’ memories and is augmented both by the subconscious minds in the Construct and the migo interventions. This can lead to dream-like scenes and unusual or unreal events happening within the construct that will unsettle and cause sanity loss for the investigators.

## GLITCHES

The construct will try to augment the investigator's memories with realistic details based on the migo’s understanding of how the world, and human society, operates. Not surprisingly this isn’t always effective and what may seem like human behaviour to a migo will appear deeply strange to a human: oddly out of fashion clothes, strange answers to questions, repeated remarks and meaningless statements, bizarre facial expressions; may all occur within the construct. It is also possible that the construct will glitch, freezing like a computer game, lagging, or scenes repeating. The Keeper should feel free to use these elements as an excuse to unsettle the investigators, but not to overdo it and distract from the main narrative. Start small and build up as the story progresses. Some examples of construct glitches follow:

- The investigators are running away from something, a chase, maybe, the ground turns to a thick, cloying jelly consistency that slows them down - penalty dice applies to any physical activity.

- Someone is seen walking in the sky, or on water, before the correct physical laws suddenly kick in and they fall to the ground, completely unharmed. Sanity Loss 1/1d6.

- Someone is seen walking down the side of a wall, gravity seems to be pulling them down, but it's like their shoes are glued to the wall, they walk to a window, and fall through it. Sanity Loss 1/1d6.

<!-- source-page-005 -->

- One of the characters wakes up in the middle of the night to find a small child squatting on the end of the bed, the child turns to them and then its face melts, before the child disappears. Sanity Loss 1/1d6.

- The characters are walking or driving down a street/road, then seconds later they find themselves walking down the exact same stretch of road they have already covered. Sanity Loss 0/1d4.

- When walking into a room, or speaking to someone, the pc gets a real sense of deja-vu - like they've been here before - but of course, they have. Sanity Loss 0/1.

## DREAMS

The majority of Construct 7 is built from the investigators’ memories of their investigation and will as a consequence have a dreamlike quality. As with construct glitches, dream sequences can be used by the Keeper to unsettle the players.

When investigators ‘sleep’ within the Construct, they will dream, but their dreams are a combination of reflections of things that are happening, or did happen, in the ‘real’ world i.e., things they witness while in the brain-canisters or before they were captured, and subconscious attempts to make sense of it all. Every time the investigators sleep, ask for an Idea roll. If they make it, they remember a dream.

These dreams are intended to foreshadow the investigators’ true predicament, and can be read out, described by the Keeper, or presented as handouts.

### The Boardroom

You find yourself sitting at a table in a large, clean, corporate boardroom. You are surprised to be here. One wall is all glass, through which you see a cityscape from a height. It’s not immediately apparent to you which city - New York, perhaps? It’s an amazing view. You look around to see your fellow investigators sitting at the table, they all look as confused as you feel. You notice there is a man standing at the head of the table. He wears a grey suit, white shirt and red tie. The man turns to you all and smiles, but his smile is distorted, like a rictus grin. His head shakes violently, spasmodically, then splits in two as something emerges from inside - a fungal growth, with spores dispersing as the flesh of the man's face rots and falls away, forming a new disgusting fungal head. You run to the door, but it’s locked. You cannot get out. SAN loss 0/1

### The Janitor

You awake to find yourself lying down, you open your eyes to see walls moving past you. You are being pushed along, you hear the squeaking of wheels, and the footfalls of whoever, or whatever, is pushing you along. Dark shapes move and shift just outside your peripheral vision, but when you look at them, they slither out of sight. The corridor you are moving through is lit by a strange, low, brown light. You try to get up but you find you cannot move. You look down to your legs but you cannot see them. You try to call out but you have no mouth. You try to scream but no one can hear you. SAN loss 0/1

### The Monolith

You wake up in your bedroom, but there is a huge, black shape standing at the bottom of your bed. From this monolith extends organic, green webbing that crawls up the bedclothes toward you. Green blood flows through the monstrosity, you can see it pulsing through its translucent veins. A strange motif, a rune perhaps, like the number ‘7’, pulses rhythmically like a heartbeat, in time with the throbbing green liquid that flows through the webbing as it closes round your throat. You try to break free but you are trapped, paralysed with fear, your body just won’t move. You lie their immobile as the life is choked out of you. SAN loss 0/1d3

### The Monster

You’re walking through the streets of a city that’s familiar to you, but you can’t quite place. It’s strangely quiet, there is no traffic noise. You realise that all the people moving around you are all faceless mannequins, moving on rails. They all stop in their tracks and turn towards you, their featureless faces look like cloth masks drawn tightly over a human head. But then their bodies begin to crack, twisting and breaking like invisible hands are snapping their limbs. The sound of the breaking bones is sickening. You want to escape but you don’t know where to run, as you're surrounded by these contorting mannequins. You see that they are now growing new limbs, their bodies contorting into new shapes. The sound of a cacophonic buzzing fills your ears, like a swarm of immense flies in your head. The mannequins have metamorphosed into strange, clumsy, immense insects that spasm and flop uncomfortably on the tarmac. SAN loss 0/1

### The Canisters

You in a room full of tins of food, for some reason you feel compelled to open them all, the first is a nice, refreshing can of peaches, and you devour them - but they don’t taste quite right, but you need to eat, so you open another can, but it’s full of rancid corned beef. You keep opening more cans, each one more disgusting than the last, maggot-ridden meat, putrid and uncooked offal. The cans are getting bigger and bigger, and you can’t stop opening them, you feel you must, because you’re desperately trying to find something. You get to the last can and open it, and at first you cannot tell what it contains - it looks like a large, pink, fleshy cauliflower, and then you realise it’s the brain of a large animal. You look away in disgust, to see a man in a grey suit watching you from the shadows. SAN loss 0/1

## THE MIGO

The migo are keeping watch on the investigators within the Construct. They are trying to discover if the investigators can remember Elsie’s location so they can find her in the real world. Their presence in the Construct is represented by a single individual, who they have created to be what they consider an inconspicuous man, but who is in fact highly conspicuous as a consequence - the human subtleties of style, manner and attitude are completely lost on the migo. The migo-man will be dressed in a smart, slightly dated grey suit, with white shirt and red tie, his hair cropped short. The investigators will start to notice this individual, normally watching them from inaccessible places.

The migo can also change the Construct to enable the investigators to find Elsie - they want them to succeed in this. To this end the migo will stop the investigators from ‘dying’ in the construct, this will be through slightly weird but explainable events - the police conveniently arriving to save them, a passing stranger intervenes, a mattress suddenly appearing below them in a fall etc.

The migo can only learn the secret of Elsie’s location if the investigators find and visit her in the Construct. If the investigators guess or figure out where she is but don’t actually go there, the migo cannot learn the location.

## KEEPER CONSIDERATIONS

While the investigators may have deja-vu or flashbacks, they have no idea that they are not investigating this case for the first time – run the adventure exactly as if that was the case. The investigators will find out their true predicament soon enough.

It doesn't matter if the investigators do not 'relive' the investigation exactly as they lived it in the real world. Memory is never truly accurate, and besides, Elsie and the migo deliberately made attempts to make the investigators forget certain aspects. In Elsie's case using hypnosis to make the investigators forget her location, and the migo to make them forget they were captured - it is the former that the migo wish to recover, and this will not work if the investigators understand their real predicament too early.

## THE ENLIGHTENED

The Enlightened is a front for the migo mind-harvest. Most of the time, and for most people who attend, it’s just an opportunity to talk, share ideas, and be supportive within a pseudo-religious context. It is very possible to spend some time there, attend meetings, and be completely unaware of what is really going on.

The migo and their human allies use the centres as a means to discover two types of people: those who will act as loyal allies; and those with mental capabilities of interest to the migo. This happens less often than supposed, for a number of reasons: one is that masses of people going missing is hard to cover up; and two, there aren’t that many minds exceptional enough to interest the migo.

The migo are particularly interested in those with strange cognitive abilities - prediction, foresight, empathy, mental influence for example - than they are with those who have high IQs or educational success. The Enlightened have a four tier hierarchy: ‘potentials’, ‘scholars’, ‘all-knowing’, and ‘enlightened’. Only the third tier - the ‘all-knowing’ - are exposed to the alien element to the organisation. Those selected for brain extraction (referred to as ‘the ascended’) can be pulled from any tier, willingly or not, and fast-tracked to their fate.

## DRAMATIS PERSONAE

### STANWICK WEBB

![Source visual from page 6](assets/d546ef251ace98df9136ea8c332883b06594c7082d32ed1ac80c4dbd915d9523.jpg)

The father of Elsie, in his mid-fifties. As a young man he served in the British Army, serving tours in Northern Ireland, and Bosnia where he took a gunshot to the knee. He recovered but as he’s got older it has become increasingly painful for him to walk and he now mostly gets around in a wheelchair. Stanwick owns a bookshop. He leads a quiet life and has minimal social interaction.

Stanwick was a changed man after Bosnia. He’d always been certain on the distinction between right and wrong, and the essential good of humanity. The atrocities he witnessed changed all that. Now he just looks after himself, and those close to him, which is really only his daughter Elsie. His relationship with Elsie's mother broke-down after he was discharged, and they divorced when Elsie was very young.

Stanwick has a somewhat apocalyptic mindset. He believes that social order will eventually fail and man will regress. He has some illegal firearms that he picked up in Bosnia and keeps in his cottage in South Gloucestershire. He has enough tinned food and bottled water to survive for a fortnight. He has not returned to his cottage since he received the letter from Elsie.

### ELSIE WEBB

![Source visual from page 7](assets/ffd474f4a049b1968c6f7b5f9068019b5cdec558e69b9a1e995aa21663395857.jpg)

Elsie has recently resigned from her job as a social worker - her strong and natural empathy means she gets extremely stressed about the sufferings of the people she helps, which is part of the reason she joined The Enlightened to try and find some peace of mind. Elsie is a self-taught hypnotist and has always had an interest in the spiritual side of life. Her empathy with people is so strong that it often feels like mindreading to those who meet her.

Stanwick’s bookshop is a three-storey building in a small terrace in an ancient part of the city called the Christmas Steps. There is a single door, and window in the front. There is no access from the sides or rear, although there is a small yard at the back of the building surrounded by high walls.

### THE SHOPPER

The sign on the door of the shop says ‘closed’ but if they try the door they will find that it is open. When the investigators enter the shop they will see a man holding a book. He will say he has been waiting to be served but no-one seems to be about. He will ask if they know when the proprietor will be back? He will leave the book on the counter and says he will return for it another day. Anyone making an Idea roll will get a real sense of deja-vu.

### UPSTAIRS

On the first floor is Stanwick’s office. Off the landing is a single door on which is pinned a piece of paper with a strange symbol on it. This is something Elsie sent him and begged him to use. If the investigators mention it to Stanwick he will explain this. He doesn't believe it has any effect, but he loves and trusts his daughter, so is doing it for her, if nothing else.

If the investigators call out on the landing Stanwick will reply and invite them to come in. A Spot Hidden roll will notice he is wearing a small pendant round his neck with the same symbol. Another thing Elsie sent to him.

### WHAT STANWICK KNOWS

Stanwick sent the email to the investigators the day after he received the letter from Elsie. He has tried to call both her landline and her mobile - both go straight to voicemail.

Stanwick will happily share the letter that Elsie sent him with the investigators. He has done what Elsie has asked regarding the strange symbols she has sent him. He is very worried about Elsie and wants the investigators to find out where she is, and to bring her to him, or at the very least to discover that she is safe and do what they can to help her. He also wants them to find out who these ‘bad people’ are. Elsie always was an imaginative girl and he’s hoping she’s misinterpreted the situation, but she came across some bad people as a social worker and he wonders if that is how she became involved in them.

She was very excited about the future. If Jocelyn is part of the group, he will ask her to pick up anything from the flat that will have emotional significance to either of them. ## LEAVING STANWICKS BOOKSHOP

### THE FACELESS MAN

On leaving Stanwick’s bookshop Spot Hidden rolls will reveal what looks like a man with a stocking mask pulled over his face peering from an upper window at the investigators. When they look again the man does indeed have a face, he nods acknowledgement and moves back from the window. Looking at all the buildings along Christmas steps from all the upper windows lots of people appear to be peering at the investigators. When they look again, they will be gone. SAN loss 0/1

### THE SHOPPER

The man who was standing in the bookshop will be outside on the steps. He will casually ask the investigators if the proprietor has returned yet. It is up to the investigator on how they respond.

### ELSIE’S FLAT

Elsie’s flat is on Gloucester Road. It is a one-bedroom flat above a launderette. The door to the flat is barely noticeable at the side of the launderette. If they have the key from Stanwick they will be able to access it without incident, although two of the Enlightened are sitting in the launderette waiting to see if Elsie returns to her flat. A Spot Hidden from one of the investigators will notice that somebody (not one of the Enlightened) seems to be repeatedly putting a coin into one of the dryers. This will stop if the investigators enter the launderette. If they ask the man why he was repeatedly putting coins into the dryer, he will reply that he was only putting in sufficient coins, to not do so would be inappropriate. This will seem like an odd response. The Enlightened will not take notice of the investigators unless they enter Elsie’s flat, when they will become very interested.

Elsie flat has a small open-plan kitchen-diner-living area, a bathroom, and a double bedroom at the back. All utilities are still connected, including her landline and wifi. In her bedroom there is a desk. Next to the desk is a wastepaper bin, in the bin is a leaflet – give the investigator Handout 3.

### ELSIE’S DIARY

Elsie’s diary is stuffed under her mattress. She realised too late that she left it in her rush to escape, but doesn’t feel it’s important enough to run the risk of returning for it. It is more a journal than a diary, and doesn’t have entries for all days. The diary will take a couple of hours to read. Initially it details her thoughts and on her work as a social worker. It’s clear it was getting her down. She writes very sympathetically of her charges, to the point where she’s feeling their pain.

## THE ENLIGHTENED WEBSITE

The Enlightened website, www.looktothestars.co.uk, is very well designed - clean, simple graphics with soothing colours - but is very light on content - essentially a repeat of the material on the leaflet. It does list all the Wellness Centres, most of them are in UK cities but some are abroad, including London, Birmingham, Cardiff, Glasgow, Manchester, Leeds, Nottingham, Lisbon, Houston, and New York. The players may be struck by how many of them there are, but the one that will be of particular interest to the investigators is the one in Bristol. This is the one that Elsie attended.

## THE ENLIGHTENED WELLNESS CENTRE

Hidden in plain sight on Colston Avenue in the Old City, the Bristol branch of the Enlightened is a neo-classical building stuck between more modern corporate constructions. Originally a church the building was purchased by the Enlightened as a suitable place for their ‘services’. Whether they visit the Centre day or night, if they look up they will see the migo-man standing by a window straightening his tie. He will glance toward the investigators but will not acknowledge them.

<!-- source-page-011 -->

![Source visual from page 11](assets/ba5b7c947407c8409b5353e7a25196a98be9e9dc47a786ee0376cd8bc23348b4.jpg)

The front door is accessed by an iron gate that is locked at night - a locksmith roll will unlock the gate, or it could be forced with an appropriate tool, the gate could be climbed with a Climb roll. The front door would require a Locksmith roll to open or a Hard Strength roll to force, but the front of the Wellness Centre is on a very busy road, so the investigators need to be careful not to get spotted.

During office hours, and evening meetings, anyone can walk into the building, there is a friendly receptionist who is happy to share the Enlightenment leaflets and to answer questions. The receptionist is always of the ‘scholar’ tier and is a genuine enthusiast for the group. There will be a timetable of events that they will encourage the investigators to partake in, with the following headlines:

- Transhumanism - is it possible for the human mind to exist without a biological substrate?

- Neural Connections - why memory can't be trusted.

- Hypnotherapy - unlocking your true potential by selective editing of your mind

- Dreaming - are dream worlds just as real as the waking world?

The receptionist will look to register the investigators for these events, taking names and contact details. This will perhaps make the investigators nervous.

The investigators are free to wander round the ground floor of the building, there is a library, a relaxation room, and a cafe. Any attempts to wander upstairs will be politely prevented. Persistent or aggressive behaviour will be dealt with by security.

The first floor contains a library with more interesting tomes, some of them mythos, and a reading room with a bar. There is also a locked office. Within the office are details of the 4 tiers of the organisation, mention of a fast-track for ‘ascension’. Details of a place called The Institute in Wiltshire, which seems to be a head office of sorts. Anyone identified for ascension - a list on which if they search through it they will find Elsie’s name - is taken for assessment to The Institute. Elsie is the only one to be identified for a while.

Access to the first floor is only allowed for those who attain at least ‘all-knowing’ status - an option that the investigators don’t have enough time for. The other alternative is by breaking in, something that could be achieved by climbing over the ten-foot wall at the back of the building, and breaking through the side door. ## STANWICK’S COTTAGE

Just under an hour's drive from Bristol, in South Gloucestershire, Stanwick’s cottage is a two-bedroom building set within two acres of land. Situated at the end of a halfmile drive off a country road, the Institute sits in beautiful countryside, surrounded by a discreet, but protective, wall and fence. At the end of the drive there is a gatehouse where a guard sits and he will open the gate to anybody with legitimate business at the Institute - including the investigators if they can come up with a plausible reason for being there. He will even open the gate for an implausible reason, but the investigators won’t know this.

However the investigators approach, and whatever time of day, they will see the migo-man standing at one of the upper floor windows. He is drinking tea and will stare straight at the investigators. He will place the cup on the windowsill, straighten his tie, and disappear from view.
