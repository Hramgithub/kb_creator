Lights, Camera, Action!  It is 1923 and the era of silent film is in full swing.
‘The Innsmouth Terror’ will be a full-length three-act moving picture of a
glamorous dame kidnapped by a tentacled monstrosity. In Technicolor!
The Innsmouth Terror is a classic 1920s scenario for Call of Cthulhu
7th edition, perfect for those looking for a pick-up-and-play scenario set in
Arkham County. Designed for three to five players, it can be run over one
to two sessions (4-8 hours).
The booklet contains:
- A scenario designed for all levels of experience;
- Staging advice for running at conventions;
- Full colour handouts;
- Five pre-generated investigators;
- An additional print-friendly version.
This scenario can be run as a stand-alone, or as a sequel to Stage Fright at the
Playhouse by the same author.
