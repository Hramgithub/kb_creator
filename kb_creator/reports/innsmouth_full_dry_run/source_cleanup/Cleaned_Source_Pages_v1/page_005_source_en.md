INTRODUCTION
Lights, Camera, Action!  It is 1923 and the era of silent film is in
full swing. ‘The Innsmouth Terror’ will be a full-length three-act
moving picture of a glamorous dame kidnapped by a tentacled
monstrosity. In Technicolor!
But when a valuable tiara is loaned to, and then summarily
lost by, the production company, the investigators are sent
to find it. They meet a disenfranchised film crew plagued by
delays, a high-strung starlet desperate to get filming over with,
and a new-fangled camera that keeps breaking. The sense of
simmering boredom and frustration grows as the film crew find
little mirth or distraction in the creaking town of Innsmouth.
Just a sea of stoic, salty, scornful faces wishing these city folks
would leave them in peace.
Can the investigators uncover the truth before the stakes get
raised and the Innsmouth Terror shifts into something more
horrifying than what is captured through the lens of a camera?
“We shall swim out to that brooding reef in the sea and dive down through black abysses to Cyclopean and many-columned
Y’ha-nthlei, and in that lair of the Deep Ones we shall dwell amidst wonder and glory for ever.”
—H.P. Lovecraft, The Shadow over Innsmouth
PREPARING FOR PLAY
The Innsmouth Terror borrows heavily from the story The
Shadow over Innsmouth (1936) by H.P. Lovecraft and utilizes
some of the extended world building from Chaosium’s Escape
from Innsmouth (1992). However, knowledge of these books is
not required to play in, or run this investigation.
This scenario is intended to follow on from the scenario Stage
Fright at the Playhouse, itself a sequel to Edge of Darkness.
However, the scenario stands on its own and can be thought of
as sharing a universe and some NPCs with these other scenarios,
rather than being a direct sequel.
Short on time? The investigation can start in Innsmouth
(Investigation, page 13). You’ll just need to quickly explain
how the investigators got to this point.
