Genevieve ‘Ginny’ Dubois, age 25, starlet

In bad light, one might mistake Ginny’s too-wide eyes and
stretched-out smile for innocent beauty. But once the lights are
shining and the cameras are rolling, only the thick makeup and
dramatic angles can hide her slow transformation into a hybrid.
She is determined to ‘hit it big’ before her transformation takes
hold and will go to any lengths to make it so. Born in Innsmouth
to a loving mother and a terrifying, Deep One father in 1897.
It was assumed that one day, Ginny would join her brothers
and sisters down below. However, Ginny had other plans. After
running off with the family gold to study Theatre, she bought
her way into various productions at the Miskatonic Playhouse.
Sponsoring the productions in exchange for the leading role.
However, the shows all bombed. She has now attached herself
to the lead role of The Innsmouth Terror.

Ginny has milky-white skin, and wears far too much makeup.
Her bleached blonde hair is up in curls; her bright red dress is the
same colour as her lipstick and she smells faintly of lavender. A
closer look notes that slightly wide-set eyes, the slightly cracked
skin and the slightly oversized teeth.

P.A. Bazzledon, age 40, director & conman

Phineas Arthur Bazzledon is an up-and-coming director straight
out of New York. Or at least, that’s who he claims to be. With a
tailored suit, a shiny top hat, and an amaranthine waist coat, he
stands out in the crowd. A white man, sporting an impeccably
groomed mustache that sits atop a strong, square jaw so often
associated with power and leadership. The light touch of gray
appearing in his hair only adds to his effortless sophistication.
He rolled into Arkham a few weeks ago and started throwing
money around the local acting scene like it was water. It wasn’t
long before he had the entire dramatic scene circling around
him in a death spiral of devotion. When he announced his plans
to shoot a silent movie in color, he had his pick of local talent.

Bazzledon is not who he appears to be. A con artist, in his
youth, he joined a cult worshiping Dagon, thinking to make a
quick buck. But he found that his oaths to Dagon were not easily
broken. At night his dreams are wracked by Mother Hydra, the

. leader of the Deep Ones. She has instructed him to return the
child, Ginny Brewster to Innsmouth and to sacrifice her above
the town. The line of credit he is using to fund this shooting is
entirely phony and it is only a matter of time before he is hunted
down by one of the many loan sharks he has leaned on to funnel
money into this moving picture. But by then, he plans to be well
on his way to his next town, his next mark, and his next con.
