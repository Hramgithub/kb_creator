Dramatis Personae
Christopher McGuire, age 37, engineer
A tall, gangly looking white man with curly ginger hair tucked
under a flat cap, Christopher McGuire is an awkward soul who
only seems to come to life when he is working with technology.
Incredibly tall and perennially hunched over his camera, he owns
a small workshop just outside the grounds of the Miskatonic
University. He has been known to give the occasional guest
lecture to the Engineering department, though less frequently
these days, as none of the academics are quite sure what he’s
up to.
It was McGuire that pitched the idea of filming in technicolor
to Bazzledon not long after he arrived in Arkham. And Bazzledon
seized on the idea as the perfect long con.
Sadly, the technology really isn’t ready for a full-length film.
It isn’t really ready to be taken out of the lab. The camera keeps
breaking, prompting multi-hour pauses during filming where
production grinds to a halt. McGuire is doing everything he can
to keep the process going.
McGuire is desperate to make the film work and has invested
all his savings into this endeavor. He needs the film to be
completed and will keep the camera rolling to the very end.
Anna Díaz, age 26, producer
A technically brilliant, funny, well read, hard-working actress,
Anna long ago gave up on her chances of ever making it big. The
starring roles go to elegant, blond-haired bombshells, not solid-looking farm girls with dark brown skin and acne scars.
However, her near encyclopedic knowledge of the Arkham
scene and her ability to sort out just about anything made her
the perfect hire for producer of The Innsmouth Terror. Not that
Anna has ever produced a film before, but she’s done plenty of
stage shows and it can’t be that different, right?
It was Anna who sourced the old train cart that the crew are
living in. She got it at a steal, and it’s far comfier than the tents
that the crew would normally sleep in when on-site. It’s also
been a complete life saver now that production is dragging on
day after day, as there are no decent hotels in Innsmouth.
Working with a ‘cinematic genius’ like Bazzledon has been
difficult for Anna. His demands are often unexpected and unlike
anything Anna would ever imagine. However, she is just happy
to be learning from someone with so much experience. And
mostly she’s learning on the job, figuring things out herself.
Even if that’s not exactly what she expected to be learning.
Anna didn’t get to where she was by not seizing every
opportunity presented to her, so she’s going to make this
production a success, if she has to drag it kicking and screaming
across the line.
