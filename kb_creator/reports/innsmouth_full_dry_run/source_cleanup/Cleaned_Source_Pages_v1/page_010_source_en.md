Dramatis Personae

Sammy Brewster, age 21, dilettante & sibling
A perfectly pleasant young white man from a rich family, Sammy
is just starting to show the infamous Innsmouth look: his too
large eyes and slightly fishy features make him more boyish than handsome; his slick blond hair gives off the impression of being
slightly... damp.

Sammy has been hanging around the production camp trying
to speak to his sister, Ginny. A couple of days ago, he was caught
by the crew, who gave him the black eye he’s now sporting on
his right eye. Ginny has made no move to contact him. She’s just
not interested.

The Crew
An unruly mob of heavyset men and women that do the lifting,
the organizing, and occasionally play secondary roles in the film
such as ‘Scary fish-man number 2’. They can mostly be found
hanging around the production train or doing a variety of jobs
around the set. Infinitely interchangeable and of indeterminate
number, the Crew enjoy getting drunk and hanging around a fire
pit in the evening. A simmering tension exists between the Crew
and the residents of Innsmouth, and more than one fight has
broken out between the groups.

MINOR NPCS

Diane Loring

The diligent head of research at the Orne Library. She helps the
investigators in their search through the archives and point them
towards the Arkham Advertiser. She is a serious, dark-skinned
woman in her late 40s with naturally graying hair. Diane Loring
is courteous to the investigators, though stops short of being
friendly. If any investigators are students, they'll be aware of
Loring’s fierce reputation. She is always willing to help students
with serious questions, but she dislikes noise or complications
in the library.

Henry Armitage

The famous Head Librarian of the Orne Library may already
be known to the investigators. He may choose to help the
investigators in place of Diane Loring.

Karen Jeffries

The slightly tetchy, opinionated, bossy head of the Arkham
Advertiser, Karen is a firm believer that people never show her
enough respect and will absolutely not have time for a group
of investigators who think their investigation is more important
than the news. However, if they sweet talk her or seem useful,
she will fall over herself to help them out.

Franc Turner

Janitor of the late Miskatonic Playhouse. Franc is a slightly
graying Black man wearing blue overalls and sporting an
impressive mustache. He is tall and broad shouldered and
barely fits into his shirt.

Jeremiah Brewster

Second highest ranking priest in the Esoteric Order of Dagon.
He keeps the residents of the town firmly under control so as not
to upset the Deep Ones. His transformation into a Deep One is
almost complete.

Pete

Proprietor of the Innsmouth General Store. He is cheery
and friendly to the investigators. He clearly doesn’t get much
business and is glad to see some new faces.

Zadok Allen

The infamous drunk from Shadow over Innsmouth. He knows
the full story of Obed Marsh, his deal with the Deep Ones and
the three oaths the people of Innsmouth were forced to swear.
