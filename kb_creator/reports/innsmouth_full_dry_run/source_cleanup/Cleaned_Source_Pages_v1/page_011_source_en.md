ORNELIBRARY (MISKATONIC UNIVERSITY)
Our mystery opens in the quiet surroundings of the Orne Library
in the Miskatonic University. The Society for the Exploration
of the Unexplained (SEU) normally only meets once a month
in a private reading room, but this unscheduled meeting has
been quickly pulled together due to a request coming in from
the Vice Chancellor of Miskatonic University themselves! The
Vice Chancellor requires their help in recovering the Atlantean
Jewel, a bizarre looking tiara made of alloyed gold (Handout
1-a: letter). It is the only one of its kind and an archaeological
treasure. The note from the Board says little, other than to
encourage the investigators to contact Diane Loring for
further information.

As events transpired, Henry Armitage, the Head Librarian,
simply unlocked the glass case and handed the artifact over
to P.A. Bazzledon after he provided a signed note from the
Vice Chancellor of the University stating that he had been given
permission to take the item. The note was, of course, a forgery.
An internal review will eventually find Armitage was tricked
into providing the artifact and dock him one week’s wages.
Investigators may be tempted to try and find out who was
responsible for lending out the Jewel to Bazzledon. If they do so,
they find the university staff incredibly uncooperative. Armitage
is well-loved and his employees fiercely protective.

The investigators’ first port of call is most likely to be Diane
Loring, the diligent Head of Research, who works at the main
desk.

Loring explains that the Atlantean Jewel is a treasure that
was housed securely in the Orne Library behind a thickened
glass case. Its exact history and precise origin are the subject
of some fevered debate in certain archaeological circles. Most
scholars believe that it was forged by an ancient, now lost,
civilization, hence the rather uninspiring, name for the artifact.
It was ‘erroneously’ lent out to a production company for the
filming of an experimental film. The production appears to be in
Innsmouth, a small town northeast of Ipswich. No major roads
go to the town, and the only rail connection was through Rowley
closed a few years ago. She believes there is a bus service to
Innsmouth that leaves at 9pm from Dyer Street.

+ Investigators asking how the artifact came to be let
out get a dismissive glare from Loring. She explains
that she believes phony credentials were provided to
an ‘important’ member of the library, who handed the
artifact over in good faith.

+ If asked how the university found out about the theft,
Loring responds that the producer Anna Diaz reported
the loss of the Jewel to the University.

Keeper note: it is very common for Investigators to come
up with elaborate theories and ask questions that are not
contained within the bounds of the scenario. If they do ask
a question to which there exists no obvious answer, feel
free to improvise an answer that ideally leads them back to
information relevant to the scenario.
