THE ARKHAM ADVERTISER
While the Orne Library may be the ideal place for delving into
the past, nowhere can quite beat the upbeat Arkham Advertiser
for the latest news and gossip around town. Its proprietor,
Karen Jeffries, is perhaps, the most self-important gossip
in town. If the investigators arrive during the day, there is a
flurry of activity in the new room as Jeffries and her employees
sit around one large desk, talking excitedly on their phones,
chain smoking cigarettes, passing notes between each other, all
while intermittently typing away at their typewriters. The place
is vibrant and bustling. Investigators attempting to interrupt
Jeffries before she is ready to speak to them get an earful and are
told to wait to the side, where a set of incredibly uncomfortable
green chairs sit. However, if they wait patiently, Jeffries turns
her attention to them after what seems like forever.
- On a successful Archaeology roll, investigators pull a
little more information from Loring about the artifact. It
is a tiara of an unusual gold alloy, one that modern science
is unable to replicate. Such treasures are occasionally
pulled up from the sea floor by fishermen, who then go
on to sell them. This one is vaguely associated with the
town of Innsmouth, which is why it was likely taken.
- Loring is not aware of any of the details of the film or
its crew and recommends that investigators contact
the local paper The Arkham Advertiser (adjacent)
to find out more. She further mentions that, of course,
the resources of the Orne Library are available to them
should they need it.
Investigators may take many different avenues of research
when using the Orne Library. To uncover general information
about Innsmouth and the artifact, they can use a general
Library Use roll. They may also use different avenues of
research to come to some general conclusions about the theft:
On a successful Occult roll, investigators are aware that such
artifacts are often highly prized by cults and secret organizations,
who often revere items from lost cultures.
On a successful Anthropology roll, investigators intuit that
the most likely people to steal a cultural treasure are those that it
originally belonged to. They have this rather unusual belief that
the treasure somehow belongs to them.
On a successful Law roll, investigators think it’s probably
best to just follow the money. Who are Bazzledon Productions?
And would they benefit from selling the item?
For more up-to-date information, they would need to talk to
people or visit The Arkham Advertiser (adjacent).
Keeper note: the description of the Jewel will be familiar
to any player who has read The Shadow Over Innsmouth,
and the Keeper can be relatively open with the players
that the tiara is clearly linked to Innsmouth. The Jewel
was made by the Deep Ones and traded to the people of
Innsmouth as part of their ongoing deal. Tiaras like the
Atlantean Jewel are often worn by priests of the Order of
Dagon or melted down to produce gold.
Keeper note: if the investigators have met Karen before,
then she may react differently to them. Karen is best
approached with offers of large sums of money to support
the work of the Arkham Advertiser. Failing that, she is
quite shallow and likes those with high Credit Rating or
Appearance scores.
