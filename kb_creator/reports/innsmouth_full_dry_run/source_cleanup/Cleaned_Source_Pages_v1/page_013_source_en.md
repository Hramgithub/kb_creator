Investigators asking Karen for help get nothing for nothing,
and Karen must be convinced with a successful Charm or
Persuade roll, unless they bring her an exclusive story about
how the Orne Library lost a priceless artifact. That interests her
and she is then willing to share what she knows about the various
players if the investigators are willing to provide an anonymous
quote that would bring the great Miskatonic University into
disrepute.

Once Karen does open up though, she is absolutely brimming
with gossip about The Innsmouth Terror.

+ The Director, P.A Bazzledon, appeared in Arkham about
three weeks ago. He came up from New York, where his
name is associated with a number of off-Broadway hits.
It seems he’s a bit of a rising star.

+ Bazzledon has been absolutely throwing money around.
Jeffries would love to know how he made his fortune;
she’s written multiple articles about the mysterious
sources of this theatrical dilettante and rumors abound.
Everything from family money, to piracy, to striking
gold in the west has been suggested. However, the man
remains an enigma.

+ The project is apparently to be filmed in ‘Technicolor’.
The claim is that this produces real color! Jeffries
published a piece about the inventor Christopher
McGuire recently. He’s an oddball that occasionally
works with the University.

+ The star of the film is one Ginny Dubois, who recently
had a run of shows at The Miskatonic Playhouse
(adjacent), before it burned down in mysterious
circumstances. Her acting skills are ‘limited’ but Jeffries
has taken a shine to her. (On a successful Psychology
roll, investigators realize that Ginny donated money to
the Advertiser in exchange for positive reviews. Actually,
it’s pretty obvious on a failed roll, too.)

+ The producer purchased an old rail sleeper train and
had it driven out onto the abandoned rail line from
Innsmouth to Rowley. The whole crew are staying there
as the town of Innsmouth apparently lacks hotels of
sufficient quality.

If asked about Innsmouth, Jeffries makes a face. Apparently,
nothing of importance has happened in Innsmouth in a hundred
years. The only paper, the Innsmouth Courier, went out of
business back in the 1840s. She considers it almost beneath her
to even discuss it. Though, and her voice drops into a whisper,
rumors have it that the starlet Ginny Dubois is, in fact, originally
from Innsmouth. Though she claims she’s from Newburyport.

THE MISKATONIC PLAYHOUSE

Investigators may have connected Ginny Dubois to the
Playhouse and go there searching for clues. Sadly, the Miskatonic
Playhouse has burned down. As the investigators stand outside
the wreckage of this once proud building, the only person visible
is an older Black man, who appears to be cleaning in amongst
the wreckage. This is France Turner, the handyman of the
Playhouse.

Franc remembers Ginny Dubois as the starlet of the show. He
describes her as ‘trouble’ and he’s glad to be rid of her. Ginny
seemed to have one aim in mind, and that was to get herself
to Hollywood and become a star. She didn’t seem to care about
who got in her way or why. If investigators pass a Social roll
with Franc, he admits that he heard rumors that all her money
came from her family, and that they’d sent some lawyer after her
attempting to get it back.

Keeper note: if the Miskatonic Playhouse did not burn

down in your run through of Stage Fright, then it burned

down soon afterwards in another mishap. Never fear

though, it will shortly be rebuilt! O
