Díaz doesn’t want to prejudice the investigators’ inquiries and
wants them to consider all options. That being said, there are
only three places she has been unable to search:
- P.A Bazzledon’s office: “But why would he steal the
Jewel, he is furious that it’s missing, and he’s the one that
went to such trouble to get it for filming anyway.”
- Ginny Dubois’ trailer: “But she literally comes from
money. I can’t think of one single reason why she’d
engage in petty theft.”
- Christopher McGuire’s lab: “He absolutely forbids
anyone from entering there. And for good reason. That’s
where all the delicate technology is stored.”
Her only other lead is that there has been a young man from
Innsmouth hanging around the set. Most of the locals have
been staying well clear of the production, but this one has been
spotted amongst the train cars more than once. Some members
of the crew ran him off a few nights ago. Díaz suspects he is their
strongest lead.
The investigators are also, of course, free to speak to everyone
on the crew. This morning they are doing some ‘re-shoots’ out
on the field beside the train tracks, so everyone will be in one
place.
Díaz is overtly helpful, detail oriented, and entirely honest
with the investigators. She will do anything, within reason, in
her power to help the investigation. With a successful Social
roll, investigators can even convince her to ‘accidentally’ leave
the spare keys for the various train cars on the desk when she
heads out. Otherwise, investigators can attempt to snatch them
with a Sleight of Hand roll, or sneak back into the office and
take them with a successful Stealth roll.
THE PRODUCER’S OFFICE
It is early morning; though the sun is attempting to pierce its way
through, it will be hours before it can burn off the mist blanketing
the Manuxet valley. Anna Díaz sits at her crowded desk, retrofitted into a sleeping compartment of the Production Train. The
smell of fresh coffee fills the room, the window is open, and
birdsong can be heard in the distance. The investigators crowd
into the remaining space of the room. There’s not enough room
for them all to sit, and only one other chair.
Díaz thanks the investigators for coming and apologizes again
for losing the Atlantean Jewel. She understands that it is a
treasure of utmost value and notes how incredibly thankful the
production was that the University agreed to share it with them
in the first place.
As soon as she realized that the Jewel was missing, she notified
the University and conducted a thorough search. In retrospect,
she is embarrassed to admit that she immediately suspected the
Crew. Which isn’t very class conscious of her. She had the Crew
search through each other’s personal effects and paired them up
to look through the entire train. It’s not like the Jewel is small.
So she is fairly certain that it is not hidden on the carriage.
Díaz is unsure of where the Jewel was taken from and no-one
seems to know where it was being stored. Bazzledon believed it
was in McGuire’s Lab. McGuire thought it was stored in a safe
in Bazzledon’s office.
