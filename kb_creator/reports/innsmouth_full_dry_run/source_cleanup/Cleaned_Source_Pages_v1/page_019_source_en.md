Ginny’s Trailer

Ginny’s trailer is bright, the windows open, catching a little of
the sea breeze, intermingled with that fishy scent that seems to
follow everything in Innsmouth. Alongside these familiar scents
are spikes of strong perfumes. At first, the investigators might
think that the place has been ransacked. Clothes cover every
conceivable surface and the only clear floor space is around a
large mirror, surrounded by incandescent bulbs. Even here, the
discarded mascara brushes and dried-up make-up sponges are
piled up and messy. It’s only possible to infer the location of the
bed, as it is effectively just a pile of clothes at the far end of the
room, almost a nest.

Underneath the mess, a fragmented car-crash of pink, frilly
cushions and blood-red coverings are lazily arranged over
modern, expensive-looking furnishings. As the investigators
step in and around the mess, they choose to stand near the
windows to survey the room, as the mixture of scents is
drastically unpleasant.

Investigators trying to open the windows up further or passing
a Spot Hidden roll find a scribbled note attached to the frame
(Handout 2: note).

If the investigators don’t find the note on the windowsill,

they instead find Handout 2 dropped into the trash can in one
corner while searching the room.

The only things that are not clothes in the dressing room are a
few scattered frames of Ginny posing with minor local celebrities
and a framed news article from the Arkham Advertiser telling
the ‘tragic’ story of the burning down of the Miskatonic
Playhouse, tragically cutting short a celebrated (three star) run
of Woman in Bronze fronted by the ‘rising star’ Ginny Dubois.

Investigators can look at the wall between Ginny and
McGuire’s rooms. On a successful Track roll, they note light
marks and folds in the cardboard, indicating this wall has been
pulled back before. It’s difficult to tell which side the marks
come from.

There is nothing else in the room of value or interest, and
investigators can quickly make their exit.

If the investigators don’t bother searching the trailer, or fail
to enter, have them spot Handout 2 flapping against the trailer
window frame as they pass by.

Keeper note: LLFRFL are directions (Left, Left, Forward,
Right, Forward, Left) to be taken through the Caves. If
the investigators enter through the tunnel by the rail
station, these instructions take them to an opening by the
Brewster Residence (page 20).
