The Railway Entrance
A set of foreboding looking sharp rocks fall against one
another creating a natural arch. Stepping through, a small
grassy area with a lightly trodden path leads towards a
slotted hole that runs down into some naturally forming
tunnels. If investigators saw the glint of light while On Set
(page 17), their eyes dart towards the broken binoculars
lying on a rock. Otherwise, it requires a Spot Hidden to
notice the binoculars.
If investigators have found Handout 2, they can
follow the map instructions from here through The Cave
System (below). It leads to The Brewster Residence
(page 20). If they have no instructions and choose to
enter the caves anyway, they exit the caves through an
abandoned house in central Innsmouth.
THE CAVE SYSTEM
Sitting underneath the town of Innsmouth and its
accompanying cliffs, are a warrenous set of tunnels that
have been used by smugglers and deep ones alike to move
around unseen for generations. Moving through the caves
is awkward. There are no directions, just large, jagged
rocks that have been occasionally cleared to create rough
pathways through the stony labyrinth. Minor additions,
such as cut handholds and the occasional ladder suggest
routes, but frequent cave-ins mean that not all pathways
are trustworthy, or lead somewhere.
Investigators with an idea of where they are heading may
make a Navigate roll in order to try and head that way
through the caves. On a success, they arrive where they
meant to. On a failure, they come up in the entirely wrong
place (normally an abandoned home in Innsmouth).
Throughout the scenario, investigators may return to
the cave system many times. The caves can lead to The
Brewster Residence (page 20), Stumbling onto Set
(Page 21), Into Innsmouth (page 22), and The Esoteric
Order of Dagon (page 24).
Keepers should make sure they are aware of the light
source investigators are using (they likely have a lantern
with them), who is holding the light, and in what order
they are moving through the cave system.
Moving Through the Caves
Whenever the investigators are moving around the
cave, have them roll 1d100 and use Table 2 (page 19) to
generate an encounter. Roll at least two encounters each
time they traverse the caves. If investigators roll the same
result twice, take the entry below the one they rolled.
The standard way for the investigators to move into
the cave system is by finding the entrance by the railway
tracks.
However, there are many entrances to the caves, and
the Keeper should be aware that finding an entrance to the
cave system is an excellent way to move the action along if
the investigation goes off in an unusual direction. In this
case, they can emerge into Stumbling onto Set (page
21).
