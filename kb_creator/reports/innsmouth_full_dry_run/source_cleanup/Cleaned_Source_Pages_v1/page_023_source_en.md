Table 2: encounters in the caves
1D100
Description
1-5
What Luck: The Investigators discover a quick and easy route to wherever they are trying to get to. It
feels like a great little caper scampering up and down between all these rocks.
6-10
Footprints: The investigators spot fresh footprints, clear as day. On a successful Track roll, it’s clear they
were made by someone wearing feminine shoes, likely of a similar build to Ginny or Anna.
11-20
A Cracked Clue: Investigators uncover a discarded piece of metal on the ground. Clearing it of dust,
they realize it has a similar sheen to the pictures of the Jewel. But it’s the wrong shape. On a successful
Appraise roll, they determine it is gold alloy, likely valuable.
21-30
A Foreboding Beach: The investigators get turned around and find themselves on a foreboding beach
front. They’ve taken a wrong turn and the weather outside is getting worse. They can see a storm brewing
directly over the half-submerged island, just offshore from Innsmouth. They’ll need to roll again.
31-40
A Hole in the Wall: There is a hole in the wall and some dim light on the other side. Just big enough
for someone to peep through with their eyes. Any investigators looking through is confronted with an
unforgettable image of eyes and tentacles and teeth, it is confused and horrifying, prompting a Sanity roll
(1/1D4 loss)
41-50
A Blocked Path: The way forward is blocked by rubble. While investigators with a SIZ under 60 can crawl
through, larger investigators must succeed on a DEX roll or take 1D4 damage from a sharp rock.
51-60
A Crumbled Cavern: There is a gap in the cave floor, a fishy smell rises up from it. While the gap is too
small to fit down, an investigator could get stuck in it. Investigators need to pass a Jump or Climb roll, or
risk falling in. Helping each other gives bonus die. Investigators getting stuck take 1D3 damage.
61-70
A Rancid Odor: Investigators gag as the tunnel is filled with the most vile stench. A smell of eggs and
mold and death closes their windpipes. Investigators must succeed on a CON roll or take 1D3 points of
damage as they cough up their insides.
71-80
Appalling Sounds: The horrendous sound of some unnatural creature noisily eating can be heard
coming through the walls. It sounds as if it is ripping flesh from bone. This prompts a Sanity roll (0/1D2
loss)
81-90
Sudden Slap: As if from nowhere, something long and slithery whips out from between two large rocks
and slaps at whatever light source the investigators are using. Everything suddenly goes dark, prompting a
Sanity roll (0/1D3 loss). The investigator holding the light has a penalty die on the roll.
91-95
Watching Eyes: The investigator at the back of the group could swear they saw glowing eyes watching
them from the shadows. They make a Sanity roll (0/1D4 loss) as they try to convince the others they are
being followed.
96-100
Close encounters: Stepping out onto a ledge, below the investigators the cave fills with water. Some
creature is swimming down there lazily in the water. The investigators must quietly cross the ledge or
risk disturbing the Deep One in the water. One investigator must pass a Stealth roll for the group or they
must find another route and roll again. On failing the Stealth roll, a Deep One emerges from the water and
attempts to eat the investigators.
