If the investigators choose to snoop around the Brewster
Residence either before or after talking to Sammy, they find a
dilapidated house, with only a few rooms in use. Most of the rest
of the house is covered in dust. Investigators that get too nosy
risk waking a Shoggoth living in the basement (page 31), and are
forced to run for their lives.
If the investigators look around the ground floor, they step
into a pink frilly room with expensive-looking red furniture
and a comfortable looking bed in one corner. The wardrobe lies
open and clothes cover the small sofa and bed as if someone has
recently rummaged through all the clothes. This is Ginny’s old
room. Hidden underneath the bed, investigators discover the
Atlantean Jewel.
Held up close, the Jewel is intense to look at. A weird, noncongruent pattern of intertwining golden lines forming up the
shape of a tall tiara. A lightly-colored gem sits at the very-top.
It does not look like something a human would make, though it
vaguely reminds investigators of the sea.
If the investigators find the Jewel while Sammy is present, he
gets very worried, telling the investigators that item ‘probably
belongs to Dad’. He gets very excited before attempting to
snatch it and run off with it. The investigator holding it must
succeed on a Hard DEX roll in order to stop him. Sammy then
runs off into the caves, prompting a chase scene that can lead to
The Epic Finale (page 25).
Otherwise, the investigators may re-enter the caves, and
experience the Stumbling onto Set scene (page 21), or if they
have already experienced that scene, then the storm reaches its
apex and they get turned around, exiting in the center of town to
experience The Epic Finale (page 25).
THE BREWSTER RESIDENCE
Investigators can arrive at the Brewster Residence by emerging
from the cave system. If they do, the exit is inside a mundane
gardening shed in the front garden. Alternatively, they may find
the address after speaking to Zadok Allen (The Fire Station,
page 23).
In front of them stands a slightly dilapidated, though clearly
still occupied, three-story house. Around the outside, the
garden is overgrown and untended. On a successful Track roll
investigators pick up a lightly worn track leading from the garden
shed to a back window that doesn’t seem to shut properly. This
window leads into Ginny’s old room.
Knocking on the front door, a young man answers. He has
that fishy, Innsmouth look that so many of the residents have,
though they are not so striking or advanced that he would look
out of place on a university campus. This is Sammy Brewster.
He has fading bruises around one eye.
Sammy is polite, earnest and completely honest with the
investigators. Inviting them inside, he apologizes but says his
father is unavailable. He then makes a rather poor attempt at
preparing and serving tea in the front room. The inside of the
house is surprisingly clean, though more than one corner and
nook lies abandoned to the rot and dust. The house was designed
to be occupied by a large family, as well as two full-time staff
members, who would live in the rather spacious basement.
However, these days it is only Sammy and his father Jeremiah.
Jeremiah Brewster spends his days at The Esoteric Order
of Dagon (page 24), where he is an odd mix of a religious leader
and mafia boss, keeping the residents of the town firmly under
control so as not to upset the Deep Ones. Jeremiah is aware of
Mother Hydra’s order to sacrifice his daughter and has spent
considerable time and energy trying to convince her to come
home, where he could at least have made the death quick and
painless. However, when the time comes, he leads the chant
that reverberates through town as the people of Innsmouth give
praise to the fragment of the god emperor Cthulhu that graces
them with its presence.
However, Sammy is unaware of all of this. To him, his sister is
back in town and he’s hoping to catch up with her. He was only
eleven when she left, and his father never saw fit to share that
Ginny stole from the family. He headed up to the train, but was
intercepted by the Crew, who ran him off. He’s tried leaving her
multiple notes, but she doesn’t seem to have gotten them. His
priority is simply seeing her again.
Investigators interviewing Sammy find a friendly, honest, and
earnest young man. His father’s position has shielded him from
the uglier aspects of the town’s history, and he seems at peace
with, and genuinely looking forward to, his future below the sea.
Why wouldn’t he be? His life in town has always been wonderful
and his recent interactions with regular humans have been
rather unpleasant, further cementing his own loyalties.
Keeper note: in most cases, the investigators will recover
the Atlantean Jewel knowing that it is Ginny that stole
it; however, it might be that they recover it but still don’t
know if it was Sammy or Ginny that stole the Jewel. In this
case, the investigation may continue with the Stumbling
onto Set scene. However, once the culprit is identified, The
Epic Finale scene should begin.
