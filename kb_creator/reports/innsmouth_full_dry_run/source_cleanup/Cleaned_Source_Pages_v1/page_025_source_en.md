STUMBLING ONTO SET

Bright lights pain the eyes of the investigators as they round the
corner into a scene of chaos.

In front of them, Bazzledon is shouting orders, McGuire is
turning the camera arm, recording film. Behind a set of wooden
sticks that might pass for jail-cell bars in bad light, Ginny is
being dramatic, while a set of frog-like creatures poke and prod
at her with primitive looking spears. The scene is disorienting
for the investigators, whose eyes need a moment to adjust to the
bright lights.

Bazzledon is desperately trying to get Ginny to look towards
the camera and act frightened. Ginny instead wants to turn away
and sashay her hips back and forth suggestively for the camera.
Various compromises are tried before there is a sudden popping
sound from the camera and film starts flying into the air, while
McGuire grabs at it.

The tension in the room deflates. Bazzledon collapses into a
director’s chair, Ginny stamps off to a quiet corner and the Crew
deftly remove their frog suits and sit down to play some dice.
This is not the first time the camera has broken and everyone
knows they are in for a long wait while McGuire works to fix it.

The investigators now have some time where they can
interview the different NPCs, ask them questions and attempt
to get useful information from them. If the investigators are
still trying to figure out who stole the Jewel and why, they can
question the various suspects, trying to establish where they
were when the Jewel was stolen.

+ Bazzledon claims the Jewel was normally in his office,
but he left it with McGuire for ‘safekeeping’ while he
went to the grocery store. In reality, he was down in
Innsmouth speaking to members at the Esoteric Order
of Dagon.

+ Ginny claims the Jewel was always stored in Bazzledon’s
office and she hasn’t seen it. In reality, she stole it from
McGuire's lab by sneaking through the wall.

+ McGuire doesn’t remember Bazzledon coming to see
him that day, but honestly, couldn’t say for certain. He
also didn’t hear anything unusual that night. This is
because he doesn’t pay attention.

In reality, Bazzledon had in fact left the Jewel with McGuire.
Ginny then stole it from the lab and took it to her old room in
The Brewster Residence (page 20) because she didn’t want
to wear it.

Investigators may have other questions, and the Keeper
should reference the Dramatis Personae for a more complete
breakdown of each NPC, and how they might answer different
questions. It is important to note that both Bazzledon and Ginny
lie frequently and easily.

If they have recovered the Jewel, the investigators can brandish
it aloft and receive huge amounts of praise from Bazzledon who
promises them a ‘great’ reward for the successful recovery,
attempting to take the Jewel from them. If the investigators hold
on to it, this provokes a stand off with the Crew. Investigators
may flee into the tunnels, or hand it over. If the investigators
return the Jewel here, then Bazzledon is wearing it during The
Epic Finale.

If the investigators accuse Ginny of the theft, she makes a large
scene, screaming at them, provoking a stand-off with the Crew,
who eventually lead the investigators off, either voluntarily, or
aggressively. If the investigators instead accuse Sammy of the
theft, everyone agrees it must have been the ‘Innsmouth boy’
and thanks the investigators for recovering it.

Eventually, McGuire gets the film going again and the NPCs
are no longer free to talk to the investigators. While they can
continue to watch filming, nothing of interest happens.

Keeper note: if the investigators have only recently left

the Production Train, it may feel too early for them to

Stumble onto Set. Instead, have the investigators emerge

Into Innsmouth first and come back to this scene.
