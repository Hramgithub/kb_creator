The Fire Station
Sitting on 100 Paine Street, not too far from the railway, the fire
station isn’t immediately accessible to the investigators as they
encounter a dangerous looking bridge made of rotting wood that
doesn’t seem safe to cross. While it feels fine if the investigators
cross it one at a time, anyone with a SIZ greater than 70 must pass
a Luck roll or the rotten wood snaps, dropping the investigator
into the Manuxet river, taking 1D6 damage and washing up three
blocks down. The bridge certainly couldn’t hold the weight of a
car, or the one operational fire engine, putting large portions of
town at risk if the fire department was ever required (not that
the damp, rotten wood of Innsmouth would necessarily burn all
that well.)
Investigators looking for Zadok Allen find him here, resting
himself against the stone outer wall of the fire station. It looks as
if no one is manning the station today and he is all alone.
Zadok is grumpy, difficult, and uninterested in talking to a
group of strangers at first. They need to loosen his lips with a
bottle of whisky. However, if the investigators do tempt him with
a tipple, he leads them away from the station, to a nice sheltered
house on the edge of town looking out towards the cliff face.
Zadok Allen is the infamous drunk from The Shadow over
Innsmouth, and knows the full story of Obed Marsh, his deal
with the Deep Ones and the three oaths the people of Innsmouth
were forced to swear. He shares a fragmented, confused story of
what happened with the investigators, similar to what a Call of
Cthulhu Keeper may share if they were trying to recite the story
of The Shadow over Innsmouth from memory.
- When sharing the story of Innsmouth with the
investigators, Zadok focuses on the fact that the Deep
Ones punish those they feel have broken the oaths. And
rumors abound that ‘something big’ is going to happen
to an oath breaker tonight.
- If asked about Sammy or Ginny, he replies “Jeremiah’s
kids? Sammy & Ginny Brewster, right? Good kids. Good
family.’ He mentions a townhouse just off Lafayette
street, The Brewster Residence.
- If the investigators describe the Atlantean Jewel to him,
he shushes them and tells them not to talk about the holy
symbols of the Esoteric Order of Dagon in public. ‘They’
could be listening.
Eventually, Zadok grows too jumpy, staring out at the sea, and
when the Keeper feels the time is right, Zadok screams, “They’ve
heard us,” and bolts away from the investigators. If they follow
him, they eventually find him hiding inside a ruined house not
far from the Fire Station, crying and completely unresponsive.
