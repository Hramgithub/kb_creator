The Esoteric Order of Dagon

Speaking to Sammy or Zadok may lead the investigators to
believe that the Jewel is connected to the Esoteric Order of
Dagon. This is wrong; however if investigators get hooked into
this pattern, the Keeper can encourage it, having them visit the
Esoteric Order of Dagon.

Sitting prominently on Federal Street in the center of town,
this neo-classical building is hard to miss. Four Grecian
columns frame the main doorway, though the whitewash has
faded to a dull gray. Above the door, a large stone carving of the
words ‘Esoteric Order of Dagon’ stands prominently across the
building. This building is a converted Masonic temple and is by
far the grandest building in Innsmouth.

Four heavy-looking Hybrids lounge around the entranceway
and they will stop the investigators if they try and approach
the door directly. The Hybrids have a strong ‘Innsmouth Look’
about them and are quick to use a slap or gut punch to move
on any investigators that seem overly inquisitive about temple
matters.

Investigators circling the building realize that there are no
windows or other doorways other than the front entrance.
However, with a successful Natural World roll, they notice
that behind the temple, the way the rocks fall indicate there
might be a way in through the Caves. Investigators deciding to
keep watch on the building for a while must pass a Stealth roll
to not get moved on by the watchful Hybrids. If they do manage
to watch the building, they notice the same person leaving the
building twice in a row about an hour apart. There must be
another way inside.

If investigators are unable to enter the building and wish to,
the Keeper may consider a Know roll to lead the investigators
into the Cave System, looking for another way inside.

Entering through the Caves
The underground entrance to the Esoteric Order of Dagon
through the Cave System is not guarded. It is mostly used as a
way for Deep Ones to quickly enter and leave the temple without
having to walk out in the open, and the Deep Ones see no need
to guard their pathways.

As the investigators step out into the basement level, the small
stone room is empty, apart from a number of storage boxes.
The rotten smell of fish and seaweed is almost enough to make
someone gag. Investigators hear a croaking kind of song coming
through the doorway. On a successful Listen roll they hear the
words: “Cthulhu fhtagn!” repeated over and over.

Investigators peeking out from the doorway see a large room
of worship, similar to many churches they have seen. At the
front of the room stand three statues. The largest in the center
is of Cthulhu, a maddening head of tentacles, piercing eyes and
unfathomably large wings that seem to spread out to engulf
the entire building. Two smaller statues of Dagon and Mother
Hydra flank the main statue (fish-like quadrupedal creatures
with terrifying teeth. One looks built like a fighter, the other has
wide-hips, a motherly figure).

A fish-like man (Jeremiah Brewster) seems to be praying and
singing the song they hear. On his head sits a tiara of gold alloy
that looks identical to the Jewel.

Patient investigators may sit and watch. After five minutes,
Jeremiah finishes his chanting and walks towards the room
where the investigators are. They could easily ambush him at
this moment, or while he is singing. However, Jeremiah is a
powerful Sorcerer and any loud actions draw the attention of
dozens of Hybrids.
