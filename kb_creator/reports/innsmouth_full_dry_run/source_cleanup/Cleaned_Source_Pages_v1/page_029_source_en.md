Once the investigation nears completion, and the investigators
have a clear understanding that Ginny Dubois is the culprit, then
the Epic Finale begins. This could happen anywhere around
Innsmouth, though it is best for the moment to happen whilst
the investigators are in town.
Keeper note: have the investigators failed to identify
Ginny as the culprit and failed to pick up on all your
wonderful clues? Or have they instead pissed off the locals
and been captured? Don’t worry, just start the Epic Finale
anyway.

The promised storm finally washes over Innsmouth and rain
begins pouring down over the half-rotten buildings. What little
sun remains is covered by thick black clouds and the world
noticeably darkens around the investigators. Looking up to the
cliffs above Innsmouth, the investigators see a camera, pointing
at a woman dressed all in white. Ginny Dubois looks as if she
has been tied to a large stone on the very edge of the cliff, her
hands bound above her so that her white body makes a striking
X-shape against the darkened stone.
In sleepy Innsmouth, the residents pour out onto the streets.
Where just a few moments ago, they were abandoned, now
they are thronged with people, all staring up towards the
cliff face. Regular humans stand shoulder-to-shoulder with
twisted hybrids and fully transformed Deep Ones. Meanwhile,
in the shallow bay, the waves are roiling and investigators see
hundreds of yellow eyes peering out from just under the water.
Every single person’s gaze is fixed upon that white X above the
cliffs. This all prompts a Sanity roll (1/1D8). Investigators
going insane here may choose to roll on the regular bout of
insanity table or instead choose to be caught up in the moment
and follow along with the crowd as they stare and chant.
A slow chant begins. It can first be heard from those nearest
to the Esoteric Order of Dagon. Before slowly rolling out across
all of Innsmouth.
“Ph’nglui mglw’nafh Cthulhu R’lyeh wgah’nagl fhtagn Iä! Iä!
Cthulhu fhtagn”
If the investigators continue to watch, the roiling clouds
become thicker and thicker out over the shallow reef just outside
Innsmouth Harbour. And somehow, in a trick of the light, the
clouds are suddenly a mass of tentacles with a set of piercing
eyes at its center. A Star-Spawn of Cthulhu hangs over the bay.
Every single resident in Innsmouth drops to their knees in silent
prayer to this shard of a god.
The Star-Spawn’s visage is hidden behind the clouds, and the
investigators may have already decided to run, so they likely do
not fully comprehend the thing in the clouds However, if they
persist on peering past the clouds (and are not already insane)
then viewing the Star-Spawn directly prompts a Sanity roll
(1D6/1D20).
At this point, investigators may go off in many different
directions. Some possible resolutions to the story are below, but
if the investigators choose a different path then events continue
to unfold around them no matter what.
The Last Train out of Innsmouth
Investigators choosing to take flight, either through the crowds,
or the caves below have the gaze of the Star Spawn fall on them,
and it attempts to apprehend them, sending the citizenry and its
pseudopodia to capture them
If they flee through the crowds, hands grab at them from
all directions and the press of people quickly pins them down,
suffocating them. They are tied up and offered up to the Star
Spawn as further sacrifices. Move to Playing the Hero (page
27).
If the investigators take to the caves, they are instead pursued
by mindless, tentacled creatures that crawl rapidly through the
caves. The investigators spend three rounds being pursued. If
they fail to pass a DEX roll, then 1D3 tentacles attack them
(Brawl 50%, 1D3 damage). Investigators can also argue for
different rolls as they rush through the dark, dangerous caves.
Erupting out onto the edge of town, the investigators see Anna
Díaz and three surviving members of the Crew busily packing up
their belongings and loading it up on the train.  The wind is now
blowing a gale, and they all determinedly avoid looking southeast towards the Star Spawn. Díaz screams into the wind as they
approach, asking if any of them know how to drive a train.
The firebox of the train has been kept alight, and until recently
a member of the crew was cooking eggs on the hot metal.
Investigators passing an Operate Heavy Machinery or
Mechanical Repair roll know that they need to repressurize
the boiler by turning the cylinder cocks, and gently opening the
regulator. This requires that the investigators pass three STR
rolls and three DEX rolls within three rounds. At the end of the
third round, a mob of Innsmouth residents descend on the train
car, quickly overwhelming everyone.
If the investigators do get the train up and running, it lets
out a piercing whistle, building momentum slowly as it begins
running down the ancient track. The last train out of Innsmouth
rolls away, taking the surviving investigators and crew with
them.
Alternatively the train could be sabotaged to try and stop the
advancing horrors, or driven directly into the approaching mob
(see box, page 26).
