MVR Class B1 ‘Olmstead’, steam locomotive
MOV: 11
Build: 10
Armour for people: 1
Gauge: standard (4 ft 8 1⁄2 in)
Boiler pressure: 110 psi
Top Speed: 70 mph (110 km/h)
Driving the train requires a successful Drive Train or Operate Heavy Machinery roll. If the build is reduced to
zero, the train is out of action. Each full 10 hit points of damage decreases a train’s build by 1 point (round down);
damage below 10 hit points is ignored.
If the train’s build is reduced to half (round down) of its starting value or lower, it is impaired; one penalty die is
applied to all Drive Train (or appropriate skill) rolls.
If a train takes damage equal to its full build value in one incident, the vehicle is completely wrecked in an
impressive manner. It may explode, burn, roll or suffer some combination thereof. Anyone on the footplate of the
train, roll Luck. Success leads to enough of their remains being found to identify them. Anyone within 50m (165 ft)
of the engine takes 6D10 damage. Anyone outside of that area and not behind cover makes a Dodge roll. Failure
results in being hit with high velocity debris (2D4 damage). Alternatively, such an explosion could be caused by
dangerously increasing the pressure in the boiler, with a successful Drive Train, Operate Heavy Machinery,
or Hard Science (Physics) roll.
If a train’s build is otherwise reduced to zero by cumulative damage (i.e. in increments of less than the vehicle’s
starting build value), it becomes undriveable, grinding to a halt. Depending on the situation (and perhaps a Luck
roll), this may lead to an accident resulting in 1D10 damage for the driver and each passenger.
