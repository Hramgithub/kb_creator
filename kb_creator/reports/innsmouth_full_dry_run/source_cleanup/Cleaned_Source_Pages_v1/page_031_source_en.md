Playing the Hero
Investigators making their way determinedly up to the cliffs to
the sacrifice experience no violent retribution from the Star-
Spawn. In fact, the road clears ahead of them, as they rush up
the winding path towards the climatic moment. The same events
unfold if they are captured and brought up against their will.
As they approach, they see Christopher McGuire slowly
turning the handle of the camera. His eyes are glazed and he
is completely unresponsive. Ginny has been torn from the rock
where she was tied, leaving behind a pentagram, clearly drawn
in blood smeared across it. She is now in the clutches of the
tentacles above, screaming for help. A number of Crew members
lie in pieces around the site, their bodies seemingly torn to
shreds by tentacles whilst attempting to save her.
Bazzledon is on his knees, screaming along to the chant
from the town below. When the investigators arrive, his eyes
snap open and he turns to them, rushing over to whichever
investigator seems to most closely fit with the stereotype of
the hero who saves the day. Failing that, he moves towards the
investigator with the highest APP and casts the spell Mental
Suggestion (page 30).
“You’ve always wanted to be on the silver screen haven’t you.
I know you have, kid. This is your big moment, your time to
shine. You’re gonna be a movie star. Take this,…” He hands
the investigator a packet of Tum-Eez antacid “… run up that
tentacle, and drop it in the things mouth. It’ll overwhelm it,
sending it back to its watery grave.”
The investigator needs to pass a POW roll to resist the urge
to do exactly what Bazzledon suggests. Though their fellow
investigators may also attempt to stop them, by grappling with
them. If the investigator fails the roll, they run towards the Star-
Spawn, attempting to climb up one of the tentacles towards the
creature’s terrifying mouth.

Allow the investigator a Hard DEX or Throw roll to determine
if they manage to drop the Tum-Eez into the creature’s mouth
before they are torn to shreds and eaten by the Star-Spawn.
If they fail, then Bazzledon turns to the next investigator and
repeats the lines verbatim:
“You’ve always wanted to be on the silver screen haven’t you.
I know you have, kid…”
If the investigators succeed, they are, unfortunately, still
torn to shreds by the monster. It turns out that antacids have
no effect on the Star-Spawn. However, in achieving the final
shot, the movie is completed, and Bazzledon turns around, cuts
McGuire’s throat, and lopes off into the wilderness with the film
under his arm.
Investigators who overcome Bazzledon’s charms see him for
what he truly is, a madman, sending innocents to die. As they
turn to flee, there is a sudden calm in the storm and the chanting
ceases. With a cry of despair, Ginny Brewster is torn to shreds in
front of the silent denizens of Innsmouth. Her body is dropped
carelessly down into the ocean, not even given the honor of being
food for the god shard. This prompts a Sanity roll (1/1D6).
Investigators may flee over the open ground away from
Innsmouth, or come down to the train to find everyone dead,
torn to shreds as they made a valiant attempt to escape from
the monster on the train. By one way or another, the surviving
investigators return to Arkham.

What if they’re captured?
Captured investigators wake up tied-up on the cliff-face
and see the Epic Finale playing out in front of them. The
Star Spawn appears, Ginny tied to the rock. But instead
of trudging up and being approached by Bazzledon, their
bindings are cut loose by Bazzledon as he gives his pep talk
and hands them the Tum-Eez.
