Epilogue

Did the investigators escape with the Atlantean Jewel?
If they did, Miskatonic University is incredibly pleased with
the Society. They agree to the next 12 months funding up front.
Further, the Society is offered a much nicer room for future
meetings. One with a fireplace and far comfier seats usually
reserved for faculty.

If they did not, it is the beginning of the end for the Society.
Another important mission fumbled, the University begins
reconsidering its long-standing support. Perhaps it is time
they brought some experts in, rather than trusting a group of
amateurs.

A few weeks after the end of the investigation, an internal
review docks Dr. Henry Armitage one week’s wages for ‘failing
to properly check the credentials of an applicant’ and the matter
is put to rest.

Was the final scene of the movie filmed?

P.A Bazzledon is frantically sifting through the remains of his
office, pulling out the hidden safe (if it’s still there) and removing
the contents. He stuffs them in his pocket, snaps up his briefcase
and is out of there.

Outside, a group of tough looking thugs have driven up
from New York. It wasn’t hard to follow Bazzledon’s trail to
Innsmouth; they know he'll have the money, they just need to
rough him up a bit. As Bazzledon sneaks out and ducks into the
warrenous caves of Innsmouth, the question remains: Is there
a rough cut of the movie under his arm, or does it fall to the
cutting room floor?

If it is released, the investigators eventually hear rumors of a
hit new film taking the underworld by storm. The graphics are
so realistic, you'd swear the dame was actually torn apart. And
it’s in technicolor! The marvels of the modern age...
