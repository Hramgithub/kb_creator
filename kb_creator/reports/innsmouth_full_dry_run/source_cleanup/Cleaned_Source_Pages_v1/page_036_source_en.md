Backstory

Hugo never liked his first name, so when his school chums

nicknamed him Shrimpy due to his diminutive height, he

ran with it. Shrimpy’s mother, desperate to get him a proper

education, decided to send him to the Americas far away from

these cursed friends. But he kept the nickname anyway.
Shrimpy has effectively bought his way into the Society for the

Exploration of the Unexplained by providing all the refreshments

and soothing over issues with double bookings of the room. He’s

hoping this ‘good’ work will buy him some friends.

* Personal Description: a good-looking man of
diminutive height, Shrimpy is well-dressed and well-kept.

- Traits: tends to assume folk will fawn over him and gets
a bit confused when they don’t.

* Ideology & Beliefs: not entirely sure who he is at this
stage, as he’s only recently gained enough freedom to
explore. And so he’s exploring his own identity.

- Injuries & Scars: Shrimpy’s leg was badly broken as a
boy in a rugby match. He’s never fully recovered and has
an occasional limp when it rains.

- Significant People: Mummy, Daddy, and Nanny.

- Meaningful Locations: he rather likes the United
States as it’s not Blighty. Which is all he really needs.

- Treasured Possessions: his grandfather’s pocket
watch.
