Backstory

Patty should not technically be a student of the University. Not
that any of her fellow students realize this. She’s been attending
lectures, after all, and staying in some of the student halls. She
is a thief and a con woman who has just slipped into the world
of MU through the cracks and found that no one questioned her
right to be there. At this rate, she will graduate with a degree in
criminology next year.

Patty thought The Society for the Exploration of the
Unexplained might be a good way to grab some expensive loot as
they looked for really odd and unusual occurrences. But mostly
so far, it’s been boring meetings with unusually good wine.

- Personal Description: brown hair, brown eyes and a
forgettable face. Patty is hard to notice unless you really
focus.

- — Traits: direct and forceful. Patty Gillian can challenge a
man to hand over his wallet and make him think it was
his idea.

- Ideology & Beliefs: the world has been incredibly
unfair to me, so I don’t mind taking things when I get
half a chance.

- Significant People: Patty has never really had the
luxury of friends.

- Phobias & Manias: capiophobia—the fear of getting
arrested. Hearing a police siren terrifies her and she
struggles not to react.

- Meaningful Locations: ‘her’ dorm room is the first
permanent home she’s ever had.
