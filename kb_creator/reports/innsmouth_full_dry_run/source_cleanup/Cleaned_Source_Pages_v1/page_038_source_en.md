Backstory
Ian came to Miskatonic University on a Football scholarship,
plucked out of his sleepy hometown of Cabo Rojo by recruiters
looking to grow the game. Ian has an easy manner about him.
Sadly, a broken leg knocked him out for the season. Ian is diligent
and attends a mish-mash of classes when he can, but feels a bit
listless without the focus of Football driving him forward.

He’s come along to the society looking for somewhere to direct
his boundless energy.

- Personal Description: tall, dark and handsome. Ian
towers over his contemporaries. When not wearing his
football kit, he can normally be found in a white t-shirt
and blue jeans—the American Dream made solid.

- Traits: Ian doesn’t know much, but he knows how
to work hard. And that’s what he does. He hits the
gym most mornings, with a heavy focus on his rehab,
diligently attends lectures and takes extensive notes,
and then tries to spend his evenings practicing skills.
He’s got a bit of a knack for opening locks, which he sees
as fun little puzzles.

- Ideology & Beliefs: Ian believes in God cause his
mama told him to. He honestly doesn’t think it through
much more than that.

- Significant People: Mamma and Grandpa and his
uncles and aunts who live along the street back home.

- Meaningful Locations: the football field in his home
town.

- Treasured Possessions: his first pigskin (football)
given to him by his grandparents.
