Backstory
Tiff feels like she has to fight just to make the world take
her seriously. Everyone is always describing her as ‘cute’ or
‘pleasant’ when what she’d really like them to be saying is that
she’s ‘accomplished’ and ‘hard working’. She’s tried reinventing
herself, changing her hairstyle, working on her public speaking,
but none of it seems to add up to much.
She joined the society looking to make a contribution. Do
something positive, so people treat her like she’s important. She
just hopes that they pick her for a mission before too long.

- Personal Description: cute and smiley. With a wide
nose and copper-brown skin of her Quechua heritage,
Tiff dresses smart and professional, but the clothing
never seems to sit quite right on her.

- Traits: Tiff has never 100% gotten the hang of ‘people’
and strikes others mostly as a classic badly socialized
academic. But she thinks of herself as someone far more
consequential.

- Ideology & Beliefs: Tiff wants to be the main
character in her own story, dreaming of exotic digs in
amazing locations.

- Significant People: a little estranged from her family,
Tiff lives with her partner Susan. Tiff idolizes the idea of
marriage, even though she’s aware that people like her
aren’t allowed to.

- Meaningful Locations: Tiff lives off-campus with her
partner in an attic conversion that just feels so very cozy.

- Treasured Possessions: a colorful alpaca wool
poncho that was her fathers.
