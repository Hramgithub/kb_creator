Backstory
Maggie is the youngest daughter of Patricia and George Myers.
Her father is a Boston lawyer and philanthropist who set up his
own practice to help the working class. As the youngest of three
sisters, Maggie would regularly get into scraps and learned early
on how to defend herself. Her rivalry with her siblings propelled
her to succeed in school, where she made top grades.

Maggie graduated from Boston University after studying
Criminology and Law; however, she decided her real talents and
interest lay in the sciences. She joined Miskatonic University
last December as a Graduate student, studying Pathology and
Criminology. The Society for the Exploration of the Unexplained
has been a fun diversion.

- Personal Description: elegantly dressed and attractive, Margaret moves gracefully and draws
attention. You’d never know she can be quite handy in
a fight.

- Traits: Margaret would make a great politician. She
knows how to win people over but can be pretty ruthless
when she needs to be.

- Ideology & Beliefs: Margaret will always stick up for
the underdog and believes that justice only comes to
those who fight for it.

- Significant People: her older sisters Harriet and
Esme.

- Meaningful Locations: her family home in Boston.

- Treasured Possessions: a locket containing a photo
of her parents.

For a further scenario featuring Maggie, see Flesh Wounds by
Pete Burgess.
