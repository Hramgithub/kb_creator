Dear Students.
It will not be a surprise to the Society for the Exploration of
the Unexplained that the Board has taken an interest in their
work and that the curiosity and adventurous spirit of the Society aligns well with the value of Miskatonic University. It is due
to these factors that the Board has always been proud to sponsor
the Society’s work. We must now, however, ask something from the
Society.
A priceless artifact, the Atlantean Jewel, was loaned to a local
production company by the name of Bazzledon Productions. An internal investigation is underway to determine how exactly the film
company was able to get hold of the item. However, that need not
concern you all. The Producer of the production company has sadly
reported the jewelry missing from their set earlier this week. We
are charging the Society with the investigation and recovery of
this artifact. In addition, the Society is requested to gather any
evidence regarding the possible culprit to be handed over to the
local constabulary.
We have been highly impressed with the work of the Society to
date. We pray that you will not fail us in this important endeavor. Those from the Society who are selected may contact Diane
Loring in the Orne Library for further information.
Yours,
Professor Neil Arthur Tepp
Vice Chancellor
Miskatonic University
