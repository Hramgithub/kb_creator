Dear Investigators.
The Board thanks you for your quick response to our letter. We
are saddened to admit that we are in need of some immediate outside help and hope that we have not interrupted anything important with our hasty summons.
A few days ago, a priceless artifact, the Atlantean Jewel, was
loaned to a local production company by the name of Bazzledon
Productions. An internal investigation is underway to determine
how exactly the film company was able to get hold of the item.
However, that need not concern you all. The Producer of the production company has sadly reported the jewelry missing from their
set earlier this week. We are charging you with the investigation
and recovery of this artifact. In addition, you are further requested to gather any evidence regarding the possible culprit to
be handed over to the local constabulary.
Each of you come recommended, with stellar reputations for your
ability to operate quickly, and discreetly. We pray that you will
not fail us in this important endeavor. You may contact Diane
Loring in the Orne Library at your earliest convenience for further information.
Yours,
Professor Neil Arthur Tepp
Vice Chancellor
Miskatonic University
